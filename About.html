OUR STORY

Built on quality. Driven by trust.

Dana Auto Parts was founded with a clear purpose — to provide reliable and quality spare parts for heavy vehicles while making the buying experience easier for customers.

Over the years, we have built strong relationships with trusted suppliers and manufacturers, allowing us to provide dependable components for buses, trucks, lorries and other commercial vehicles.

Our focus has always remained the same: quality products, fair pricing and customer support you can rely on.
